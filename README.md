## My Favorite Series: The Glory

**The Glory** is a *Korean revenge thriller* series on Netflix, written by Kim Eun-sook and released in two parts (2022 and 2023).

> A story about a woman who spends years carefully planning her revenge on the people who ruined her life in high school.

### Main Characters
1. Moon Dong-eun (played by Song Hye-kyo)
2. Park Yeon-jin (played by Lim Ji-yeon)
3. Joo Yeo-jeong (played by Lee Do-hyun)

### Why I Like It
- The well-planned, patient revenge storyline
- The powerful acting of the cast
- The way it shows the effects of school violence
